INDEPENDENT SILENT ART SAMPLE - NOT APPROVED FINAL ART
Original project-authored primitive composition and generated meshes; no external model, texture or audio downloads.
Materials are editable assets in Materials; robots, free ball, pillar, repairable wall and three Buff symbols are Prefabs.
Player visual details live under Motion; editing material assets or adding child details survives normal scene loading and Play Mode.
The explicit creation command refuses existing assets/scene. It never replaces MainMenu, Arena or DashArenaGreybox, and does not change Build Settings.
Gameplay uses ArenaRulesProfile.CreateProductionV1, not mesh colliders. All sample geometry is non-authoritative and has no physics colliders.
The separate display board is a staged model/effect comparison, not evidence of natural gameplay events.
Chinese HUD references the separately approved Noto Sans CJK SC Regular font. Its source and license record are retained under Fonts.
No sound or music is included. The golden bell is a visual effect only.
Controls: local P1 WASD + current Dash (default K), P2 arrows + current Dash (default top-row 2).
