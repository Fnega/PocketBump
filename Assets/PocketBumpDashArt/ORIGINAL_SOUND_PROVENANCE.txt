PocketBump 1.0 original synthesized sound effects

Created for this project in October 2026 using the project's editable C# source:
Assets/Scripts/PocketBump/DashSoundSynthesis.cs

The Dash, impact, wall break, three pickup, goal and respawn sounds are synthesized
from sine oscillators, envelopes and a private xorshift noise generator. They use
no downloaded recordings, external audio assets, sample libraries or music.
No third-party audio attribution or runtime network access is required.

Audio clips are generated once in memory per match component and reused through
a bounded voice pool. The synthesizer never uses the simulation's random state.
Gameplay-triggered audio consumes corrected confirmed events only. Presentation
volume is stored locally and is not part of deterministic gameplay settings.

The earlier art-sample notes describing a silent preview apply to that isolated
sample. The 1.0 production game includes these approved original short effects.
The game's existing font and art licenses remain unchanged.
